Cynthia Savard Saucier

VP of Design, Shopify

Cynthia Savard Saucier is Vice President of Design at Shopify and co-author of Tragic Design, a book examining the real-world consequences of poor design decisions. With over 11 years leading design at Shopify, she specializes in UX strategy, ethical design, design leadership, and building high-performing design organizations that create meaningful, human-centered digital experiences.

Celine Mornet

Innovation Director, Moment Factory

With over 13 years at Moment Factory, Celine Mornet has grown from interactive developer to Innovation Director, leading initiatives in GenAI, Digital Twins, AR/XR, game engines, and immersive technologies. She drives innovation strategy, strategic partnerships, and cross-disciplinary collaboration while shaping the future of interactive experiences through emerging technologies.
