Ready for Impulse 26?

That's right, SAP's design festival is back! After the incredible energy and creativity of last year's event, we're thrilled to announce Impulse 26 is coming to the Design Community of Montreal.

This year's motto is the human spark.

We know AI can create fast, polished output, but the soul of design still belongs to humans. Intuition, lived experience, and sensory perception take center stage as Impulse 26 immerses you in a multi-sensory UX jam with inspiring speakers, hands-on workshops, exploratory design jams, and unconventional ways to connect.

You'll leave feeling grounded, energized, and creatively recharged.

Humans are the spark.
The tools follow.

FAQs

Will food and drinks will be offered?
Yes ! We will provide breakfast, lunch, snacks and some canapé, antipasti and drinks during the afterwork party.
What is Impulse 2026 going to be about?
This year's motto is The Human Spark! ✨

AI can generate almost anything in seconds these days, so we're turning our attention to what it can't replicate: the intuition, senses, imperfections, and lived experiences that make our work genuinely meaningful. Impulse 26 is a multi-sensory journey through the space between humans and machines: think touch, sound, movement, and hands-on creation, all in dialogue with AI.
Who is invited to Impulse?
Registration is open to employees working in design-related roles, based in and around Montreal and Ottawa, including their direct managers.
I've registered too late, can I still join?
Send us an email to make sure to secure your spot!
Will the event be online too?
No, this is an on-site only event.
